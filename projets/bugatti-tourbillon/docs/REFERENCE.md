# Bugatti Tourbillon reference sheet (PARTIAL, in progress)

## 1. Official specifications
Source A = Bugatti newsroom technical sheet "technical-specifications-bugatti-TB-en.pdf" (status 06.2024).
Source B = Bugatti press release 6/21/2024 "The Bugatti Tourbillon: an automotive icon pour l'eternite".
- Length 4,671 mm; width 2,051 mm (2,165 mm with mirrors); height 1,189 mm; wheelbase 2,740 mm (A).
- Tyres: Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2, 285/35 R20 front, 345/30 R21 rear, bespoke development (B).
- Brakes: carbo-ceramic, brake-by-wire (B). Caliper piston counts not in official sheets.
- Mass < 1,995 kg (DIN); 8.3 l naturally aspirated V16 (90 deg, 1,000 hp, 9,000 rpm); 3 e-motors (2 front, 1 rear); 1,800 hp total (A).
- Doors: electrically actuated dihedral doors, opened from key fob, a button "just underneath the Bugatti Line" and on the centre console (B).
- Rear wing remains submerged at top speed; deploys for downforce at lower speed and as airbrake (B, aero release 2/27/2025).
- Diffuser starts to climb just behind the passenger cabin; crash structure integrated in diffuser; open rear-end design (B).
- Interior: skeletonised analogue cluster, 600+ parts, titanium, sapphire, ruby, 700 g, fixed hub steering wheel (spokes reach around behind the cluster); centre console crystal glass + anodised aluminium, knurled aluminium switches, pull-to-start lever; hidden touchscreen deploys from top of console (portrait 2 s, landscape 5 s); seats fixed to floor, pedal box adjustable (B).
- Four design elements: horseshoe grille, Bugatti Line, central ridge, dual colour split (B).
