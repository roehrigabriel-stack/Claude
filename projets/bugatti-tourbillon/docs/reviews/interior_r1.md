# Interior review r1 (critic)

Renders judged: sheet_c, sheet_e, sheet_f, sheet_s. Refs: B1, B3, B4, cluster_02, wheel_console_01.

## 1. Verdict

Partly. A fan would pick up "Bugatti" from the EB hub, the three-dial skeleton binnacle, the squircle wheel, the pale-blue/slate split and the sloping glass-topped console, so the ingredients are right. The cabin as a whole does not read as the Tourbillon. The dash is a flat-topped slab with a rectangular step instead of the twin-hump "wing" with the central spine blade. The seats are thin pills. The footwell is a black void. The wheel rim is pale blue instead of dark leather. The console is a wide tray of identical buttons with chrome nubs instead of a slim blade with three big glass knobs. The pale blue is also blown out, so there is no sculpting. Closer to a good concept mock-up than to B1/B3.

## 3. Keep as is

1. Sloping blade profile of the console from the side (seat_pass_f, door_s): the slope, polished rail and lowering toward the tunnel are close to B3.
2. The skeleton-dial concept with visible gears, three-dial layout and EB logo are recognisable (cluster_c/e); keep the layout, fix the plate and scale.
3. The squircle wheel plan form, the sill plates with the BUGATTI lettering and the carbon sill, and the two-tone pale blue / slate palette direction.
